Frequently Asked Questions

Direct answers regarding rates, terms, and approval guidelines.

What is equipment financing?

Equipment financing allows businesses to acquire necessary machinery without paying the entire purchase price upfront. A lender provides capital to purchase the asset, and the business repays the principal and interest over scheduled monthly terms.

How does equipment financing work?

You select the desired machine from any dealer or private party and submit a quote or invoice. Once approved, the lender disburses purchase funds directly to the equipment vendor, and you take immediate possession while repaying across agreed installments.

What types of equipment can be financed?

Any revenue-generating commercial equipment: yellow iron, titled vocational trucks, trailers, machine tooling, curb systems, and commercial landscaping equipment can be funded.

What happens at the end of the financing term?

Depending on your contract structure, you can execute a $1 buyout option to own the equipment outright, refinance into extended terms, or return the machinery for an updated unit.

Is financing available for younger businesses or startups?

Yes. Businesses with 6+ months of history can qualify for application-only tiers. We evaluate business cash flow and equipment collateral value rather than solely personal credit scores.
